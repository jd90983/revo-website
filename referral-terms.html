Referral Program Terms

How the Revo Referral Program works: who can refer, when a referral counts, the reward, and how you may share your link.

Effective Date: November 2026

Table of Contents

  1. 1. Who can refer
  2. 2. How a referral counts
  3. 3. Your reward
  4. 4. Limits
  5. 5. How you may share your link

  8. 8. Changes
  9. 9. Contact

1. Who can refer

Anyone with a Revo account in good standing, on a free trial or a paid plan, can take part in the Revo Referral Program (the “Program”). These Program terms are in addition to the Revo Terms of Service.

2. How a referral counts

Share your personal referral link. A referral counts when a business that is new to Revo signs up through your link, or names you when it signs up and Revo can confirm it, and then pays its first invoice for a paid Revo plan. If more than one person refers the same business, the first referral wins.

3. Your reward

For each referral that counts, you get one month of Revo free: a credit on your Revo account equal to your monthly plan payment, used on your next invoice. If you are not on a paid plan yet, the credit is used on your first monthly payment. Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged.

4. Limits

  • One reward per referred business.
  • You cannot refer yourself, your own other businesses or accounts, or accounts created to earn credits.
  • The referred business must be new to Revo, with no earlier Revo account.
  • If the referred business gets a refund or a chargeback on its first invoice, the credit for that referral is removed.

5. How you may share your link

Share your link only with people and businesses you know. You may not:

  • send bulk or unsolicited email, or use bought, rented or scraped lists;
  • send automated or mass text messages or calls;
  • post your link on coupon or deal sites, or in reviews of Revo on app stores or review sites;
  • run paid ads that use “Revo” in keywords or that look like they come from Revo;
  • say you are Revo or speak for Revo, or make claims about Revo that are not on revoapp.ai.

You are responsible for following the laws that apply to your messages, including the CAN-SPAM Act and the Telephone Consumer Protection Act.

6. Say that you get a reward

If you talk about Revo in public, for example on social media, in a video or on a website, clearly say that you get a free month when someone signs up with your link, as the Federal Trade Commission’s Endorsement Guides require.

7. Misuse

Revo may hold or cancel credits, and end your access to the Program, if these terms are broken or a referral looks fraudulent.

8. Changes

Revo may change or end the Program at any time by updating this page. Credits already earned are honored.
