BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few things
to know.

What does an AI receptionist do?

Revo answers calls and collects customer requests using your script, hours and rules. Call summaries help your team review the request and plan follow-up.

Will it work for my home service business?

Start with the questions and next steps your business needs. Use the demo to confirm the supported setup for your trade, phone coverage and appointment workflow before routing real calls.

Can Revo book appointments or transfer calls?

Booking depends on your configured workflow. Revo’s published plan comparison lists team transfers in Crew and Fleet. Confirm the plan and setup for your needs during the demo.

Does Revo offer missed call text back?

Automatic missed-call text back is not a verified Revo feature. If SMS follow-up is part of your process, ask the team what is supported. This page describes AI phone answering and call summaries.

What does Revo cost?

See Revo’s current pricing and plan comparison for rates, included minutes and terms. Discuss your expected call volume during the demo.
