    Common questions about Revo's AI virtual receptionist service

    Is Revo legit? How can I verify the service?

    Revo at www.revoapp.ai is an AI receptionist service for businesses: it answers calls, collects customer details and supports appointment booking using your script, hours and rules. Before subscribing, compare the Solo, Crew and Fleet plans, included minutes and features, and review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. The Terms list Revo AI’s contact details, including (844) 523-1919 and legal@revoapp.ai. Confirm your workflow, billing and cancellation terms with the team before purchasing or forwarding business calls.

    How does Revo work?

    Revo is an AI receptionist that answers calls and collects customer requests using your script, hours and rules. The configured workflow supports appointment booking and, on eligible plans, team transfers. Revo provides a call summary with key details and next steps. Confirm the supported setup before forwarding calls.

    Which plans include team transfers?

    Team transfers are listed in Crew and Fleet; Solo focuses on core answering and booking. Review the current plan comparison on Revo’s pricing page. Ask the team to confirm the transfer destination and fallback when a team member is unavailable.

    How much does Revo cost?

    Revo’s published Launch Offer lists Solo at $87.50 per month with 100 minutes, Crew at $175 with 200 minutes, and Fleet at $350 with 400 minutes. The pricing page is the source for current rates and terms. Review the offer before choosing a plan.

    Can I customize the AI receptionist?

    Revo’s published plans include your own script, hours and rules. Define the greeting and the questions needed to handle customer requests. Review booking rules and the next steps for calls that need team attention. Test the configured script before launch.

    What happens if I use all my minutes?

    Revo’s pricing page says customers can upgrade to the next plan or contact the team about adding minutes. Confirm the applicable terms for extra usage. The monthly fee and included allowance do not establish an extra-minute rate.
