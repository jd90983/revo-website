## 3. Fictional worked example A: shared Growth minutes

Assumptions: one enabled Growth location, one monthly allowance of 100 Voice AI minutes, and all three listed usage types included in that allowance. No pooling across locations or other account behavior is assumed.

| Fictional input | Minutes |
|---|---:|
| Inbound Voice AI | 80 |
| Outbound Voice AI | 15 |
| Voice AI widget | 10 |
| Total | 105 |
| Included allowance | 100 |
| Above allowance: max(total − allowance, 0) | 5 |

The 100-minute inclusion is from [HighLevel's AI Product Pricing](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006652-ai-product), reviewed October 23, 2026. The usage is fictional. The computed result is **excess units only**, not the AI or phone bill. Selected rates, model/configuration, phone charges and the actual applicable account terms remain unresolved. No calls were made or authorized by this example.

## 4. Fictional worked example B: an agency bundle

Assumptions: a fictional agency quote expressly includes platform access and the selected AI subscription in its $180 monthly package. It expressly excludes phone charges. Additional usage or support charges are not established, so they remain unknown until the written scope resolves them.

| Component | Fictional quote treatment | Amount counted in known recurring subtotal |
|---|---|---:|
| Agency package, including platform access and AI subscription | Payable package | $180 |
| Platform access listed as included | Already included, not another payable line | $0 additional |
| AI subscription listed as included | Already included, not another payable line | $0 additional |
| Phone charges | Excluded; amount unresolved | Not calculated |
| Any additional AI usage or support charge | Inclusion/rule unresolved | Not calculated |
| Known recurring package subtotal | Incomplete bill | $180 |

Do not calculate $180 + $50 merely because an underlying AI plan has a documented price. Do not label the unknown components zero. **No all-in invoice total is established.** The fictional $180 is not an actual offer or evidence of anyone's margin.
