# Stage 1: expanded live Semrush research

Chrome personal is occupied at this stage. All Semrush access is through Chrome; no API, connector, purchase, metric-refresh button or campaign changes. Eight seeds have been read: call answering, call forwarding, conditional call forwarding, call screening, call routing, call transfer, auto attendant and warm transfer. Pay-as-you-go answering service is the next open query.

These are not yet 13 fully validated new-page opportunities: twelve still need individual SERP checks and product/content differentiation.
