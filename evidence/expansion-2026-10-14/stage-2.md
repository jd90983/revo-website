This supersedes the restrictive first-turn demand assessment. It is a shortlist, not publication approval or a guarantee that all twenty survive the SERP/product gates. US volume, KD and tool intent observed in Semrush Chrome on Oct 14. No changes to tracking records, APIs, paid actions or redirect.

Twelve still need their individual SERP check. Reminder SERP is heavily mixed with healthcare and remains conditional/lower priority. Newly measured useful subjects: business call recording 1,000/KD23; missed call text back 480/KD18; phone call answering script 260/KD13; warm transfer vs cold transfer 260/KD19 commercial; pay as you go answering service 210/KD14; ai call summary 210/KD29. Similar keyword suggestions were read but no zeroes or KD values were invented. Outbound/healthcare and unsupported named-software terms were excluded.
