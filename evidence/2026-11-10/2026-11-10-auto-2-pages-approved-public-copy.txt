# How a 24 7 Answering Service Handles Home Service Calls

A 24 7 answering service is crucial for home service businesses that receive calls outside of regular hours. It ensures every caller is greeted professionally, appointments are booked based on your rules, and no opportunity is missed, no matter the time of day.

Published by Revo, which sells an AI receptionist for home service businesses. Reviewed November 10, 2026.

## How Calls Are Handled at Any Hour
Calls forwarded to a 24 7 answering service are answered by a virtual receptionist following your business script. Whether it’s late at night or early morning, callers are greeted in English or Spanish and guided through your process. The receptionist gathers the necessary details and reassures customers their needs are being addressed.

## Booking Appointments and Following Up
Once the caller’s information is collected, the service books appointments directly into your calendar or field service software if connected. Every caller receives an SMS follow-up confirming their appointment or next steps. This keeps your schedule organized and helps reduce no-shows, even when you are off the clock.

## Keeping You Informed with Call Summaries
After each call, a summary is logged and sent to you or your team, so you always know what your customers need. For businesses using advanced features, recordings and transcripts are available. This transparency helps you stay on top of customer requests and ensures every call is handled professionally.
