# eVoice Alternatives: Phone System or Reception Service?

If you are comparing eVoice alternatives for a home service business, start with what you want to replace. A business phone number, an automated phone menu, and a person answering customer calls are different purchases. Replacing the reception task does not necessarily replace the phone system behind it.

Published by Revo, a competing provider. This comparison uses public documentation reviewed November 4, 2026. It is not a hands-on service test, and Revo is not affiliated with eVoice.

## Separate eVoice's phone plans from its live receptionist service

[eVoice's pricing page](https://www.evoice.com/pricing/) describes business phone plans. Its advertised features include business calling and number-management functions. Those plans should not be read as the price of a human receptionist answering every incoming inquiry.

The separate [live receptionist service](https://www.evoice.com/live-receptionist/) describes a US-based team, appointment handling, and service customized to the business. It describes per-minute billing and publishes availability as 8 a.m.–8 p.m. EST, with virtual handling after hours. Confirm the applicable days, holiday schedule, time-zone treatment, and handling outside that window in your proposal. The published hour label does not establish round-the-clock human coverage.

That distinction changes the comparison. If you need extensions and business calling, evaluate a phone service against those requirements. If you need someone to receive an estimate request while your crew is working, compare the reception workflow as its own scope.

## Keep the phone functions you still need

Use this worksheet before moving a number or canceling a service. These are proposed purchasing checks, not tests we performed.

| Requirement | What to establish before switching |
| --- | --- |
| Business number and outbound calling | Which provider supplies them and what remains available after the change |
| Extensions or an automated menu | Whether the replacement supports the same routing your staff actually uses |
| A new customer's estimate request | What may be booked, what is only collected, and who receives the result |
| Calls outside staffed hours | Whether a human, automated system, or AI service handles the call |
| An existing customer's unresolved issue | Who owns the follow-up and what information reaches that person |

Do not assume that an AI receptionist includes number porting, a complete business phone system, or every messaging feature in your current plan. Keep those requirements in the written comparison.

## Compare the reception offer on its own terms

Revo offers 24/7 AI reception with business instructions, appointment booking, SMS follow-up, and call summaries. Team transfers and software connections depend on the selected plan. Confirm the exact supported workflow before choosing a plan or changing your existing phone arrangement.

For eVoice's live service, request the receptionist allowance, chargeable minutes, overage terms, and coverage schedule. For Revo, review the current plan and its applicable limits. Give both suppliers the same description of the calls your office needs handled. Neither a phone-plan headline nor an answered-call count proves a completed service appointment.

Also check the terms attached to the particular purchase. eVoice's live receptionist page excludes that service from its money-back guarantee. Do not carry a guarantee advertised for a phone plan over to the receptionist add-on.

## Choose the arrangement your office can operate

Keep a phone service when its calling and routing functions remain necessary. Choose human reception when that model and its agreed coverage fit the conversations your customers bring. Consider Revo when your reception rules and supported actions fit an AI service. The useful alternative is the arrangement that preserves the required phone functions and gives each unresolved call an owner.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is eVoice's standard phone plan the same as its live receptionist service?
No. Its public pages present a business phone offer and a separate live receptionist service. Request the terms for the specific services you intend to buy.

### Does eVoice advertise 24/7 human answering on the live receptionist page?
The reviewed page publishes 8 a.m.–8 p.m. EST and describes virtual handling after hours. Confirm the complete schedule directly rather than assuming 24/7 human coverage.

### Can Revo replace every eVoice phone feature?
Do not assume full phone-system equivalence. Match your number, calling, routing, messaging, and reception requirements to the applicable offer. [Review Revo's current plans](/pricing).
