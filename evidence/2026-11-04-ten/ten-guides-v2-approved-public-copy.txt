# Virtual Receptionist Pricing: How the Models Work

Virtual receptionist pricing is easier to compare once you know which unit you are buying. Most offers fall into three models, and each one charges for a different part of the same call.

Published by Revo, which sells an AI receptionist for home service businesses. Reviewed November 4, 2026.

## The three pricing models
- **Live receptionist minutes:** you pay for the time a person spends on your calls.
- **AI minutes added to a phone plan:** you keep a phone plan and pay extra for AI answering time.
- **Flat AI reception plan:** one monthly price with an included allowance of minutes.

## Count these before you ask for quotes
Write down four numbers from your phone records: calls per month, average call length, share that arrive after hours, and share that need a person. Then ask every provider the same three questions: what happens to the bill on very short calls, on transferred calls, and when you pass the allowance.

[See all plans](/pricing).

## Frequently asked questions
### Is a virtual receptionist cheaper than an answering service?
It depends on your call mix. Compare the same month of calls under each provider's billing rules.
### Do AI receptionists charge per minute?
Some do; others include minutes in a flat plan. Ask what happens after the allowance.

# Automated Phone Answering System: Menu or AI Receptionist?

An automated phone answering system can mean two different products. A menu (auto attendant) plays options and routes the call. An AI receptionist holds a conversation and finishes the task. Choose by what most of your callers need.

Published by Revo, which sells an AI receptionist for home service businesses. Reviewed November 4, 2026.

## Menus route, they do not resolve
A menu works when callers know where to go: billing, a specific technician, an existing job. Many business phone plans include one. The limit is that someone still has to answer the routed call, or it ends in voicemail.

## AI receptionists handle the request
A homeowner with a leaking water heater does not want option three. An AI receptionist asks what happened, collects the address and books the visit under your rules. Revo does this 24/7 on every plan, with SMS follow-up and a call summary.

## A simple test before you buy
Take 30 recent calls and mark each one "knew where to go" or "needed help". If most needed help, a menu adds a step before the same problem. Many businesses use both: a menu for existing customers and an AI receptionist for new requests.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is an auto attendant the same as an AI receptionist?
No. A menu routes calls; an AI receptionist converses and completes tasks such as booking.
### Can Revo transfer to my team?
Yes, on Crew and Fleet. [See plans](/pricing).
