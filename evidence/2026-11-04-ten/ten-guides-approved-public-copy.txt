# Virtual Receptionist Pricing: Minutes, Calls or Flat Plans

Virtual receptionist pricing is easier to compare once you know which unit you are buying: live receptionist minutes, AI minutes added to a phone plan, or a flat AI plan with an included allowance. Each model charges for a different part of the same call.

Published by Revo, a competing provider. This is a documentation-based buying guide reviewed November 4, 2026, not a hands-on benchmark.

## Three pricing units you will see

| Model | What you pay for | Example from a published page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live receptionist minutes | Time a person spends on your calls | Ruby's plans differ only by the number of receptionist minutes; prices are quoted through sales |
| AI minutes on a phone plan | A phone plan plus AI answering minutes | Talkroute lists an AI Receptionist add-on (beta) from $39/mo plus $0.40/min, on top of a Business Phone plan from $19/mo |
| Flat AI reception plan | A monthly plan with included minutes | Revo Solo: $87.50/mo at launch for the first three monthly payments after the trial, then $175/mo, with 100 minutes |

Prices above were read on the providers' pages on November 4, 2026 and can change.

## Price the call, not the plan name

Before you compare quotes, write down four numbers from your own phone records: calls per month, average length, share that arrive after hours, and share that need a person. Then ask each provider what happens to the bill when a call is very short, when it is transferred, and when it exceeds the plan allowance. Ruby states it charges no extra fees for activation, onboarding or setup. Revo does not count calls under 20 seconds. Talkroute bills AI minutes beyond the included amount at its published per-minute rate. These rules matter as much as the headline price.

## When a flat AI plan fits a home service business

A flat AI plan fits when most calls are routine: a new job request, a reschedule, a question about hours or service area. Revo answers 24/7, books appointments with your script and rules, sends SMS follow-up and logs a call summary on every plan. Transfers to your team and a connection to your field service software start on Crew. If most of your calls need a person to quote or negotiate, price live receptionist minutes as well and compare both against the same month of calls.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a virtual receptionist cheaper than an answering service?
It depends on the unit and your call mix. Compare the same month of calls under each provider's billing rules.

### Do AI receptionists charge per minute?
Some do, as add-on minutes; others include minutes in a flat plan. Ask what happens after the allowance.

# Automated Phone Answering System: Menu or AI Receptionist?

An automated phone answering system can mean two different products. A menu (auto attendant or interactive voice response) plays options and routes the call. An AI receptionist holds a conversation and can finish the task. Choose by what most of your callers need when they reach you.

Published by Revo, a competing provider. This is a documentation-based buying guide reviewed November 4, 2026, not a hands-on benchmark.

## Menu systems route, they do not resolve

A menu works when callers know where they want to go: billing, a specific technician, an existing job. Many business phone plans include menus. Talkroute, for example, lists one menu on its Plus plan ($39/mo) and unlimited menus on Pro ($59/mo). The limit is that someone still has to answer the routed call, or it ends in voicemail.

## AI receptionists handle the request

A homeowner with a leaking water heater does not want option three. An AI receptionist asks what happened, collects the address and books the visit under your rules. Talkroute sells this separately as an AI Receptionist add-on (beta) from $39/mo. Revo is built around it: 24/7 answering, appointment booking, SMS follow-up and call summaries on every plan.

## A simple test before you buy

Pull 30 recent calls and mark each one: "knew where to go" or "needed help". If most knew where to go, a menu may be enough. If most needed help, a menu adds a step before the same problem. Many businesses use both: a menu for existing customers and an AI receptionist for new requests.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an auto attendant the same as an AI receptionist?
No. A menu routes calls; an AI receptionist converses and completes tasks such as booking.

### Can an automated system transfer to my team?
Revo transfers calls on Crew and Fleet. Check each provider's plan.

### What does Revo cost?
See the current plans on the [pricing page](/pricing).
